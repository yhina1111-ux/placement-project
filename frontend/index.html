      Help center

      Answers to common questions.

      How does the prediction work?

      A logistic regression model, trained on 10,000 past student records, turns your 10 inputs into a placement probability.

      How accurate is it?

      About 81% on students it had not seen, with an AUC of 0.88. It is an estimate, not a guarantee.

      Why do internships show almost no effect?

      In this dataset the model found internships nearly irrelevant. Aptitude score, placement training and extracurriculars mattered most.

      What do the bars mean?

      Green bars raised your score and orange bars lowered it, compared with an average student.

      Is my data stored?

      Only in your own browser, for your reports and trend. Nothing is saved on a server.
